RESEARCH DESIGNUS Equities / VolatilityUpdated: 2026-11-03

Timing Equity Exposure under Extreme Fear

A research protocol comparing first-spike entry, staged buying and trend-confirmed buying after equity-market stress.

Publication status: This is a research protocol, not completed empirical analysis. No model returns, statistical significance, or investment performance is asserted. It does not constitute investment advice.

1. Research question

After a large volatility spike, is it more effective to buy immediately, average in across subsequent periods, or wait until prices confirm a recovery? The research distinguishes absolute return from implementation risk and recovery time.

2. Competing hypotheses

  1. Contrarian entry: buying at an extreme volatility reading captures the equity risk premium at distressed prices, but can suffer severe short-run drawdowns.
  2. Staged entry: distributing purchases reduces sensitivity to a single entry date, potentially at the expense of participating in sharp rebounds.
  3. Trend confirmation: waiting for a positive trend sacrifices some upside but might reduce time spent in persistent bear markets.

3. Study design

ComponentProtocol
Primary assetsSPY and QQQ, analyzed separately
Stress conditionsAbsolute VIX level above 30; rolling percentile definition as robustness check
Entry strategiesFirst eligible spike; equal-sized staged purchases; trend confirmation (e.g., 20D and/or 250D moving average)
Holding horizon6–12 months and thesis/exit-rule variants; not an automatic 5/10/20-day liquidation
BenchmarksBuy-and-hold, fixed-date DCA, matched exposure, same rebalancing schedule
MeasurementsTotal return, excess return, MDD, recovery time, Calmar, turnover and cost sensitivity

Overlapping events are consolidated to avoid treating adjacent stressed trading days as independent observations. Cash drag and exposure matching are reported explicitly.

4. Falsification and robustness

  • Test the pre-specified strategy on genuinely out-of-sample periods, not just a recent regime.
  • Include transaction costs, dividend adjustments, survivorship and calendar alignment checks.
  • Vary VIX thresholds and MA lengths, but report the full parameter grid rather than selecting the best result.
  • Report event count and confidence intervals; concentrated crisis periods may make naive significance estimates unreliable.
  • Check whether conditional gains persist after controlling for trend and simple volatility regimes.

5. What would count as evidence?

Evidence would require improved risk-adjusted results or materially shorter recovery time relative to sensible baselines, across enough independent episodes to support inference. A positive average return during selected crises is insufficient to demonstrate usable alpha.

6. Research log

2026-11-03: Initial public methodological outline. Results and datasets have not been published here.

RESEARCH DESIGNChina Equities / SystematicUpdated: 2026-11-03

Cross-Sectional Momentum in CSI300

A reproducible experiment plan for short-horizon momentum signals in China's large-cap equity market.

Publication status: This is a research protocol, not completed empirical analysis. No model returns, statistical significance, or investment performance is asserted. It does not constitute investment advice.

1. Research question

Do short-horizon return signals improve selection inside a point-in-time CSI300 universe after accounting for implementation frictions, changing market regimes and turnover?

2. Test matrix

DimensionCandidate specification
UniverseHistorical CSI300 membership, effective at each reconstitution date
Signal lookback10, 20 and 60 trading days, with longer-term signals as optional controls
RebalanceDaily, weekly and monthly schedules, with portfolio drift accounted for
ConstructionRanked long-only portfolios with equal-weight and liquidity-aware alternatives
Risk controlsPosition limits, turnover control, market exposure and sector concentration
EvaluationSharpe, information ratio, max drawdown, recovery, turnover and net return

3. Data integrity

  • Apply index membership changes only after their effective date; avoid today's constituents retroactively.
  • Respect trade suspensions, price limits, delistings and differences between signal and execution prices.
  • Use adjusted total-return data where available and document corporate-action handling.
  • Specify realistic fees, slippage, minimum liquidity and rebalancing rules.

4. Identification

Benchmark against index buy-and-hold, equal-weight baskets and naive momentum; report gross and net results. Cross-check whether any apparent advantage is driven by market beta, sector exposures, or a handful of names.

5. Decision criteria

An attractive gross backtest is not sufficient. We need meaningful net incremental returns, stability across parameters and regimes, implementable capacity, and a repeatable statistical design.

PUBLIC PROTOTYPE / MARKET INTELLIGENCEUpdated: 2026-11-03

Multi-Dimensional Market Recap

A research workflow that turns cross-asset data into structured facts, then into reviewable analyst commentary.

01 / Why build it?

A market recap that only lists price changes is not sufficient for portfolio research. An analyst needs to know whether a move is broad or concentrated, whether rates and FX confirm it, and whether the commentary is supported by actual observations. The project aims to make daily reporting consistent, reusable and less dependent on manual copy-paste.

02 / The documented architecture

Ingest

Historical daily OHLCV from Yahoo Finance for equity indices, sector ETFs, rates proxies, FX, commodities, volatility and large-cap technology. Institutional email/Bloomberg integrations remain future ideas, not documented production data feeds.

Compute

Standardized daily, weekly, monthly, YTD and longer-horizon metrics, with missing observations preserved rather than invented.

Establish facts

Deterministic calculations for leading and lagging sectors, sector dispersion, curve slope, regime labels and cross-asset snapshots.

Synthesize

Section-specific LLM prompts generate narrative from the computed facts. Each step receives only relevant structured inputs.

Validate and render

Canonical JSON is checked against a schema and used to generate Markdown, email HTML and web views.

03 / Coverage versus questions

Data familyResearch question
Equity indices & sectorsIs leadership broad? Which sectors diverge?
Rates & FXDoes the macro pricing support the equity narrative?
Gold, energy & VIXHow does the cross-asset risk regime shift?
Analyst profilesWhich facts are relevant to different mandates?

04 / Product decisions and trade-offs

  • Data-first, LLM-second: the language model should never manufacture missing market data.
  • One canonical report: Markdown, email and dashboards read from one normalized artifact.
  • Failure transparency: null and unavailable feeds should be visibly identified.
  • File-based first: avoid a heavy database and service stack until user needs justify it.

05 / What is not yet demonstrated

The repository documents an evolving prototype, not a fully audited production intelligence platform. Robust live institutional feeds, verified news attribution, authenticated collaborative review and measured time savings have not been established here.

Proposed evaluation criteria include source availability, invalid-claim rate, report generation success, manual edit time and completeness of provenance. These are proposed metrics, not achieved results.

Supporting materials: public repository, pipeline methodology, and product requirements.

PRODUCT DISCOVERY / NOT SHIPPEDUpdated: 2026-11-03

Research Intelligence Workbench

A proposed AI product for transforming research documents into traceable claims, revisions and measurable forecasts.

01 / Product problem

Research teams receive information from many sources. Summaries alone do not solve the problem of verifying who said what, when it became public, whether the claim was directional, or whether it proved useful later. The intended product is a traceable research system, not another chatbot wrapper.

02 / User personas

PersonaJob to be done
Investment analystFind conflicting arguments and changes from prior reports
Research leadEvaluate accuracy and source track record over time
Quant researcherExport time-stamped, source-attributed hypotheses as candidate signals

03 / Proposed minimum viable workflow

Register

Ingest authorized source material with author, publisher and received timestamps.

Extract

Attach asset, direction, horizon and evidence spans to each independent claim.

Review

Present claims to analysts for verification rather than automatically treating generated text as true.

Evaluate

After the forecast horizon passes, measure outcomes using an explicit market-data protocol.

Retrieve

Deliver cited, time-aware research comparisons and tracked updates.

04 / Product success and safety

Proposed success measures: grounded-claim precision, percentage of outputs with valid evidence, analyst correction rate, extraction cost and update latency. These are target metrics, not published measurements.

The MVP should use public or expressly authorized documents. Employer emails, licensed sell-side materials, client information and Bloomberg data are excluded without appropriate permission and technical controls.

Status: A product-discovery concept; no live user deployments, accuracy benchmarks or completed integrations are claimed.
