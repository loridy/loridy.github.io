PUBLIC PROTOTYPE / MARKET INTELLIGENCEUpdated: 2026-11-03

Multi-Dimensional Market Recap

A research workflow that turns cross-asset data into structured facts, then into reviewable analyst commentary.

01 / Why build it?

A market recap that only lists price changes is not sufficient for portfolio research. An analyst needs to know whether a move is broad or concentrated, whether rates and FX confirm it, and whether the commentary is supported by actual observations. The project aims to make daily reporting consistent, reusable and less dependent on manual copy-paste.

02 / The documented architecture

Ingest

Historical daily OHLCV from Yahoo Finance for equity indices, sector ETFs, rates proxies, FX, commodities, volatility and large-cap technology. Institutional email/Bloomberg integrations remain future ideas, not documented production data feeds.

Compute

Standardized daily, weekly, monthly, YTD and longer-horizon metrics, with missing observations preserved rather than invented.

Establish facts

Deterministic calculations for leading and lagging sectors, sector dispersion, curve slope, regime labels and cross-asset snapshots.

Synthesize

Section-specific LLM prompts generate narrative from the computed facts. Each step receives only relevant structured inputs.

Validate and render

Canonical JSON is checked against a schema and used to generate Markdown, email HTML and web views.

03 / Coverage versus questions

Data familyResearch question
Equity indices & sectorsIs leadership broad? Which sectors diverge?
Rates & FXDoes the macro pricing support the equity narrative?
Gold, energy & VIXHow does the cross-asset risk regime shift?
Analyst profilesWhich facts are relevant to different mandates?

04 / Product decisions and trade-offs

  • Data-first, LLM-second: the language model should never manufacture missing market data.
  • One canonical report: Markdown, email and dashboards read from one normalized artifact.
  • Failure transparency: null and unavailable feeds should be visibly identified.
  • File-based first: avoid a heavy database and service stack until user needs justify it.

05 / What is not yet demonstrated

The repository documents an evolving prototype, not a fully audited production intelligence platform. Robust live institutional feeds, verified news attribution, authenticated collaborative review and measured time savings have not been established here.

Proposed evaluation criteria include source availability, invalid-claim rate, report generation success, manual edit time and completeness of provenance. These are proposed metrics, not achieved results.
