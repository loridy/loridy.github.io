Research standards

REPEATABILITY FIRST
Point-in-time data

Only information that would have been available when decisions were made.

Meaningful baselines

Passive and naive alternatives before claiming alpha.

Risk & implementation

Costs, turnover, drawdown, liquidity and recovery.

Out-of-sample tests

Robustness, failure modes and honest null results.
